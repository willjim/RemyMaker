User Operations & Guidelines Manual

📂 1. 快速入门：加载 3D 模型

📂 1. Quick Start: Loading 3D Models

系统支持从网络分享链接直接提取并展示 3DGS（三维高斯泼溅）与常规三维点云模型：

The system supports directly fetching and rendering 3DGS (Gaussian Splatting) and regular 3D point cloud models from online share URLs:

  1. 通过分享链接加载：在首页中央的 AI 对话输入框（或者在模型加载后的顶部菜单栏）粘贴 Remy3D 或 Kiri Engine 分享地址（如 https://remy3d.com/share/...），然后点击 Load 按钮。系统会启动内置代理自动解析并加载模型。
  1. Load via Share Link: Paste a Remy3D or Kiri Engine share URL (e.g., https://remy3d.com/share/...) in the centered AI input box (or in the top toolbar after loading), then click the Load button. The system automatically fetches, parses, and loads the model.

🖱️ 2. 基础操作：场景视角与常规调节

🖱️ 2. Basic Operations: Viewport & Adjustments

  • 视角旋转：在 3D 画布区域，按住 鼠标左键 并拖拽。
  • 视角平移：按住 鼠标右键 并拖拽。
  • 场景缩放：滚动 鼠标中键滚轮。系统支持极小距离缩放，可将视角拉得极近来观测模型微小细节。
  • 初始视角自动缩放对齐：当模型加载时，无论背景切割设置多大，默认视角都会精准缩放对齐到主体的 1x radius 距离，让人物面部或物体核心细节直接贴近屏幕中央。
  • 渲染引擎切换与背景裁剪规则：
    • ✨ Spark 3DGS 模式：照片级写实的高保真三维高斯着色。在此模式下，系统将自动保留 100% 完整的原始背景和环境内容（无裁剪），并自动拉伸相机的远截面（Far Clip），保证宏大背景不会被截断。
    • ☁️ Point Cloud 模式：展现充满未来科技感的荧光圆点粒子状态。在此模式下，系统默认将背景点云控制在干净的 2.5x 模型半径内，完美剔除环境噪点。
  • Rotate View: Press and hold the Left Mouse Button and drag on the 3D canvas area.
  • Pan View: Press and hold the Right Mouse Button and drag.
  • Zooming: Scroll the Middle Mouse Wheel. The system supports micro-distance zooming, allowing you to get extremely close to observe fine details of the model.
  • Initial Auto-Fit Zoom: When a model loads, regardless of the background filter radius, the default camera will accurately align and fit to a 1x radius distance of the model's centroid, placing the subject directly in the center at close range.
  • Toggle Render Mode & Clipping Rules:
    • ✨ Spark 3DGS Mode: Photorealistic high-fidelity 3D Gaussian Splatting rendering. Under this mode, the system automatically keeps 100% of the raw background environment (no cropping) and extends the camera's far clip plane to prevent distant scenes from being cut off.
    • ☁️ Point Cloud Mode: Renders glowing holographic-style floating point cloud particles. Under this mode, the background is automatically filtered to a clean 2.5x model radius to exclude messy environmental noise points.

🎥 3. 电影级运镜录制与 15 套内置闭环运镜预设

🎥 3. Cinematic Flight Path Recording & 15 Built-in Presets

点击顶部 🎥 Camera Path 开启运镜控制台，录制属于您的电影航拍视频：

Click the 🎥 Camera Path button at the top to open the flight path console and record your own aerial tracking videos:

🚀 新增：15 套 GSAP 复合型闭环运镜

我们内置了 15 套高级运镜预设（包含 Dolly Zoom 希区柯克滑动变焦、8字形无限循环、螺旋上升收缩、钟摆等）。这些预设轨迹完全基于您当前的缩放大小（Zoom）与仰角（Pitch）动态计算，且运行期间支持鼠标右键拖拽实时平移运镜轴心。

🚀 New: 15 Built-in GSAP Closed Loop Flight Paths

We have integrated 15 advanced camera flight presets (e.g., Hitchcock Dolly Zoom, Figure-8 loop, spiral ascending, pendulum). These trajectories are dynamically calculated based on your current Zoom level and Pitch angle, and support real-time panning of the tracking center with the right mouse button.

  1. 使用内置运镜预设：在 Flight Path Preset 下拉菜单中选择一套内置运镜（如 Hitchcock Dolly Zoom），点击 ▶️ Preview 即可开始预览。在预览运行期间，网页会自动锁定页面缩放防误触。
  2. 自定义记录关键帧：选择 None (Custom Keyframes) 模式下，调整视角后点击 ➕ Add Keyframe 增加第一视角（支持 2 ~ 8 个关键帧），并点击 ▶️ Preview 沿轨迹平滑飞行。
  3. 60帧 导出视频：无论选择预设还是自定义路径，点击 📹 Export 1080p MP4 都会自动启用离屏 1080p 渲染并生成匀速 60fps MP4 视频，录制完将自动触发浏览器下载。
  1. Built-in Presets: Select a flight path preset from the Flight Path Preset dropdown (e.g., Hitchcock Dolly Zoom), then click ▶️ Preview to begin. During the preview, page zoom controls are locked to avoid misclicks.
  2. Custom Keyframes: Under the None (Custom Keyframes) mode, adjust your viewpoint, click ➕ Add Keyframe (supports 2 to 5 frames), and click ▶️ Preview to fly smoothly along your custom path.
  3. 60FPS Video Export: Click 📹 Export 1080p MP4 under any mode. The system triggers offscreen 1080p WebGL rendering with a decoupled virtual timestamp step. Regardless of GPU lag, the exported MP4/WebM video is guaranteed to have a constant 60 FPS playback rate and will download automatically.

🤚 4. 核心体验：智能 AI 手势追踪

🤚 4. Core Experience: Intelligent AI Gesture Tracking

点击工具栏的 🤚 Gesture 按钮授权摄像头。开启后，在摄像头前展示特定手势即可控制场景：

Click the 🤚 Gesture button in the toolbar to grant camera access. Once enabled, display specific gestures in front of the camera to control the scene:

💡 识别小贴士

请保持手部光线充足并距摄像头 0.5 ~ 1.5 米。系统内置 8 帧防抖动缓冲和低通指数平滑滤波，动作表现非常柔和线性，不用担心手发抖导致画面乱颤。

💡 Detection Tips

Please ensure adequate lighting and maintain a distance of 0.5 to 1.5 meters from the camera. The system features an 8-frame anti-shake buffer and low-pass exponential smoothing filters for smooth, linear motion feedback.
